# Reference: Launch Sequence

*`marketing-architect` reference. Read for `launch`. Assumes `positioning.md` §2 already passed — Constraint 7.*

## Launch Sequence

### 1. Waves, Not a Push

A launch is not a day. Treat it as three waves, because one simultaneous blast wastes the two things that compound: the feedback that improves the pitch, and the baseline that makes the next wave land better.

| Wave | When | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Quiet** | 1–3 weeks before | The evergreen layer (`placement.md` §2); people who already know the project | Get listed, get the install path exercised by someone who is not you, and fix what the first strangers trip on. Nothing here is announced. |
| **1 — Narrow** | Launch week | The two or three concentrated channels for the audience | The real test. High-signal feedback, low blast radius, and this is where the pitch gets its final correction. |
| **2 — Broad** | Days to weeks after wave 1, only if wave 1 held | The large aggregator or link community | One shot. Go here with a pitch that has already survived contact and a surface that has already had its rough edges filed off. |

**Wave 2 is optional and is not the point.** A project can be healthy having never done it. Going there with an untested pitch is how the single highest-ceiling channel gets spent for nothing.

### 2. Pre-Seeding

Be a participant before you are a promoter. In each channel from wave 1, before launch week: answer questions, comment usefully, be a name that has appeared before. This is a cost in time, not in integrity — it is not manufacturing a persona, and it is not creating accounts. It exists because a first-ever post that is a link to your own thing is the exact pattern moderation is tuned for.

If there is no time for this, launch into the channels where the user is already a real participant, and skip the others. **Not launching into a community is always available.** Faking standing in one is not (`SKILL.md` Constraint 4).

### 3. The Response Window

The hours after a post lands are the cheapest lever in this file and the one most often left unstaffed.

- **Someone is present and answering**, in the thread, for the first several hours. Not a scheduled post — a person.
- **Answer the critical comments first, and concede the correct ones.** A maintainer who says "yes, that is a real limitation, here is why it is like that" converts better than one who defends. The critical thread is also the one everyone else reads.
- **Do not argue, do not vote-manipulate, do not mobilize a group to vote.** Beyond the ethics, coordinated voting is detected and penalized, and the penalty attaches to the project.
- **Log the questions.** The three things people ask twice belong on the first screen, and this is the highest-quality positioning research available anywhere.

Schedule the launch for a window the responder can actually be present for. The measured timing effect is real but bounded (`evidence-base.md`); being present is worth more than the optimal hour.

### 4. Timing

- Pick a window when the audience is at work and the responder is awake. The one study with a measurement found a real difference between good and poor hours, and no weekday/weekend effect worth planning around.
- **Do not stack waves against each other.** Two channels on one day halves the response capacity for both.
- **Never launch into something you cannot fix.** A known-broken install path, an expiring credential, an unfinished docs page — the launch is the worst possible time to discover any of them.

### 5. When It Flops

It usually does something between nothing and modest, and the medians say that is the ordinary outcome, not a failure (`evidence-base.md`). What matters is reading it correctly.

- **A flat launch is a positioning result far more often than a channel result.** Before concluding the channel was wrong, re-run `positioning.md` §2 — and check whether anyone who arrived actually stayed, which is the only signal that separates "nobody saw it" from "they saw it and left".
- **Re-posting the same thing to the same place is not a retry.** It is the behavior that gets accounts banned. The profile's launch-history row exists to make this impossible to do by accident.
- **The legitimate second attempt is a different artifact** — a real new version, a written-up result, a genuine milestone — to a channel that has not seen this project, or to the same one after a long gap and with its rules re-read.
- **Do not escalate to paid or to volume.** Link 1 or 2 of the funnel floor is failing, and neither responds to spending.

### 6. The Rollback

Launches have one-way doors too, and they are named before the post, not after.

- **What cannot be undone:** a post that was seen, a ban, a comparison that misstated a competitor, a claim quoted back at you later, a number you projected. Nothing on this list has a rollback — which is why Constraints 1, 3, and 4 are absolute.
- **What can:** the post can be deleted, the listing can be corrected, the surface can be fixed. Do those quickly and say what changed.
- **If a factual error shipped**, correct it in the thread where it was seen, plainly, without a defense. `module-evidence` §1 applies to a post exactly as it does to a report.
